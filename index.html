Units & numerical model

Length L, mass M, time T; G = 1 L³/(M·T²). Fixed-step velocity Verlet, Δt = 0.005 T. Gravity is softened over 0.5 L to keep close encounters finite. High speeds and extreme masses reduce accuracy. Collisions include crossings within each step.
